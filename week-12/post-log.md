🚀 Week 12 of #100DaysOfML Complete!

My first dive into Unsupervised Learning! This week I tackled Customer Segmentation using k-Means Clustering and PCA.

Project Highlights:
• Grouped a mall's customer base into distinct purchasing segments without any prior labels.
• Used the Elbow Method to mathematically determine the optimal number of clusters.
• Applied PCA (Principal Component Analysis) to crush high-dimensional data down to 2D so I could actually visualize the customer segments on a scatter plot.
