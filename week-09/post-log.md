🚀 Week 9 of #100DaysOfML Complete!

What's better than one decision tree? A hundred of them voting together. This week I studied Random Forests and predicted Employee Attrition (IBM HR Analytics).

Project Highlights:
• Built an ensemble model to predict whether an employee would leave the company.
• Used the Random Forest to extract Feature Importance, discovering exactly which factors (like salary, commute distance, or overtime) drove attrition the most.
• Bagging and random feature selection mathematically solved the overfitting problems I had in Week 8!
