🚀 Week 11 of #100DaysOfML Complete!

Taking a step back to instance-based learning! I used k-Nearest Neighbors (kNN) to build a Wine Quality Classifier.

Project Highlights:
• Predicted whether a wine is 'good' or 'bad' based on its physicochemical tests.
• Learned firsthand why Feature Scaling is absolutely mandatory for distance-based algorithms like kNN. Without scaling, variables with large numbers completely hijacked the distance calculations.
• Plotted the error rate against different values of 'k' to find the sweet spot.
