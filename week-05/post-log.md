🚀 Week 5 of #100DaysOfML Complete!

This week was critical. I learned Support Vector Machines (SVM) and, more importantly, why Accuracy is often a terrible metric. I applied this to Breast Cancer Detection.

Project Highlights:
• Trained an SVM to classify tumors as malignant or benign.
• Focused heavily on Recall instead of Accuracy. In medical diagnosis, missing a positive case (False Negative) is catastrophic. 
• Generated my first Confusion Matrix to visualize exactly where the model was making mistakes.
