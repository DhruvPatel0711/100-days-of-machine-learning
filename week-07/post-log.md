Before jumping into complex models, I spent this week studying exactly how models fail. I built an Overfitting Demonstration Model.

Project Highlights:
• I intentionally overfit a model to training data until it memorized the noise.
• I plotted the learning curves to visually show the exact moment the validation error started increasing while the training error dropped.
• Explored the Bias-Variance tradeoff in practice.
