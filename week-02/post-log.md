🚀 Week 2 of #100DaysOfML Complete!

This week was all about Linear Regression, and I applied it to my first real project: House Price Prediction.

Instead of just looking at the math, I built a model from scratch to predict housing prices based on features like square footage and neighborhood. 

Project Highlights:
• Performed EDA to find the strongest predictors of house prices.
• Trained a baseline Linear Regression model.
• Evaluated the model using RMSE to see exactly how far off my predictions were in actual dollars.
