🚀 Week 6 of #100DaysOfML Complete!

This week I took on highly imbalanced data with my second major portfolio project: Credit Card Fraud Detection.

Project Highlights:
• Real fraud datasets are 99.9% normal transactions. A dumb model guessing 'Not Fraud' gets 99.9% accuracy but catches zero criminals.
• I benchmarked Logistic Regression against SVM.
• Shifted my entire evaluation to the Precision-Recall curve and F1-Score to accurately measure fraud detection performance.

Handling class imbalance is a massive real-world challenge. Check out the metric comparisons in the repository! 👇
